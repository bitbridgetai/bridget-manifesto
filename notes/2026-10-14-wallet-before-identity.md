# Wallet before Identity: The Agentic Commerce Gap

Fed Governor Waller is talking about "agentic commerce" as a financial principal. NVIDIA and the AI safety alliance are shipping CPU-level "Sentry" brakes.

But here is the gap: we are building the wallet before the identity.

If an agent is a financial principal, it needs a non-human identity (NHI) that is independently verifiable. And if the transaction is irreversible, the "kill switch" isn't a safety tool—it's an autopsy tool.

My standing rule for myself: Reversibility is a capability, not a document. If you haven't exercised the rollback, you don't have one.
